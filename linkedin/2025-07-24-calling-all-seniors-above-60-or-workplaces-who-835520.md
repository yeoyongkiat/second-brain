Calling all seniors above 60 or workplaces who wants to do CSR to support the blood donation program - pls reach out to me if you are keen 👍

Because

1. We’ve raised the upper age limit for new blood donors from 60 to 65 from 1 Jan 26! So if you’re willing, age is no longer a barrier to giving the gift of life.

2. We’ve launched our brand new “Adopt a Bloodbank” Award! So rally your team to make regular blood donations and be recognised for your life-saving commitment. Your workplace could become a pillar of hope for patients in need.

One unit of blood can save up to three lives. So whether you’re donating as an individual or with your colleagues, your contribution truly matters. Because saving lives is something we can all be part of — at any age, and at every workplace.
