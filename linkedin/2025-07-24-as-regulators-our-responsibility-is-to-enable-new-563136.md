As regulators, our responsibility is to enable new innovations such as AI and ensure that AI in healthcare enhances—not undermines—safety, accountability, and trust.

So we are happy for Singapore, as the first Asian country, to join in HealthAI - The Global Agency for Responsible AI in Health’s Global Regulatory Network to learn, co-create, and lead together with the international community.

These will be done through (1) knowledge sharing and promoting the adoption of best practices in the development, deployment and use of AI solutions; (2) training, capability development and staff exchanges, as well as (3) building governance toolkits such as the global repository of validated AI solutions, and global early warning networks for adverse events related to AI devices. With these, we aim to ensure the safety and effectiveness of AI solutions developed and used globally and built trust amongst the developers, healthcare institutions and consumers.

Many thanks to Ricardo Baptista Leite, M.D. Yi-Roe Tan Ivan Koh Sandra Chua Shawn Tan Nicholas Nghai for making this partnership possible! MOH and Health Sciences Authority definitely look forward to partnering with HealthAI and the other pioneer countries to implement and advance the governance and regulation of AI in the healthcare sector.
