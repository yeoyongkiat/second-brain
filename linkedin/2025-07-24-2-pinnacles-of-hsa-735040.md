#2: Pinnacles of HSA

A Big Congrats to our 29 HSA staff who was awarded the Singapore Health Quality Service Awards this afternoon as we celebrate their pinnacle achievements! On behalf of the awardees, I was honoured to represent HSA and accompany them to receive 3 Star, 20 Gold and 6 Silver Singapore Health Quality Service Awards from President Tharman for their outstanding quality care and excellent patient experience. These awards are indeed a testament to all their dedication, awesome work and stellar service quality in their interactions with blood donors, public enquiries and feedback, health product and laboratory industry stakeholders, healthcare professionals and other governmental agencies 👏👏

Continue keeping up all your great work 👍👍 and exemplifying HSA core values in serving the nation, inspiring trust and striving passion for excellence, so as to make a meaningful difference to the organisation and work we do!

Also kudos to the other 4,700 awardees from 45 public and private healthcare institutions and organisations from the Community Care sector for their professionalism and excellent confributions to the healthcare sector too 🥰🥰 it was also a good opportunity to have caught up with many friends in the healthcare sector after the ceremony!
