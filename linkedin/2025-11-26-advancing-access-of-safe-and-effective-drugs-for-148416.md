🌍 Advancing Access of Safe and Effective Drugs for Over 150 Million People Across the ACCESS Region

As members of the ACCESS Consortium — Australia, Canada, Singapore, Switzerland and the United Kingdom — we are all united by a common purpose: to enable faster, trusted, and more efficient access to safe and effective health products for patients.

Our shared commitment to governance, reliance, openness, worksharing and harmonisation continues to transform ACCESS as one of the top choices for the industry to file in, ensuring that regulatory excellence translates into meaningful health outcomes for more than 150 million people across our jurisdictions.

Together, we’re strengthening global partnerships, transforming regulatory science, and driving access that makes a difference.

We are launching a manifesto and thereafter engaging closely with the industry and the associations, including Singapore Association of Pharmaceutical Industries, to see how we can work together to make a difference!
