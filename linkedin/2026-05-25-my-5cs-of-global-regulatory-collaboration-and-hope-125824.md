My 5Cs of global regulatory collaboration 😊😊 and hope it resonates!

Firstly, very honoured, Zili Li, MD, MPH and Marwan Fathallah, to have invited HSA in Singapore to be on this global panel of regulators at the opening of DRUG INFORMATION ASSOCIATION (DIA). Indeed we are very small but hopefully we are value creating in the global regulatory landscape and with our industry stakeholders too.

I summarised my 5Cs at the end of this panel session (not the 5Cs Singaporeans know) but our meaningful regulatory progress globally depends on these 5Cs:

🔹 Collaboration – Building trusted partnerships across regulators, industry and academia
🔹 Convergence – Aligning and harmonising standards and approaches to reduce duplication
🔹 Consistency and Certainty – Providing predictable and transparent regulatory pathways and outcomes
🔹 Complexity Reduction – Simplifying processes, including using technology and AI, to accelerate innovation
🔹 Capacity and Capability Building – Investing in people, regulatory sciences and institutional excellence

Together, these 5Cs create a more connected and effective global regulatory ecosystem—one that ultimately increases access to innovative health products for patients around the world.

As healthcare innovation becomes increasingly global, regulation must evolve from being a national function to a collaborative international enterprise.

When regulators work together, innovation moves faster, companies gain greater confidence, and patients benefit sooner.

Excited about what lies ahead for HSA in advancing our regulatory partnerships , reliance and work sharing schemes and in becoming one of the growing network of trusted regulators that regulators will want to work with ans where industry will consider filing their first in class or best in class products here in Singapoee.
