“Love is not only something you feel, it is something you do” - David Wilkerson

Valentine day is not just for lovers - it’s also about celebrating love in all forms and expressing love to our friends, colleagues and partners who give us their wonderful love, care and support. Your friendship, camaraderie and professionalism has created a loving environment where we all thrive and are proud and love to be part of, when we make a difference to the lives of the citizens we serve.

So today, I decided to take some time off to distribute roses to the ladies (and red KitKats to the gentlemen) and express my heartfelt appreciation to HSA staff for their hard work and dedication (some at the public facing blood banks and many at the backend forensic scientific, corporate and regulatory roles) b, as well as to the blood donors for their unwavering support of the good cause 👍 it was gratifying to see their smiling faces and joy receiving their first roses of the day or year ☺️☺️

Thank you to all my HSA friends, partners and donors for being the hearts at HSA 🥰🥰 and it’s not only on V day but every day of the year!
