My first “amazing “ feat last weekend when I delivered a 120 min lecture and Q&A fully in Mandarin to a group of 20 senior Chinese healthcare officials on “Singapore’s Evolving Healthcare Regulations: Balancing Innovation and Safety of New Technologies and Services” 😅😅 “讲解详细清楚,受益良多，期待合作” were some of the positive feedback, so, I was both relieved and glad it went pretty smoothly and we had rich exchanges and discussions for possible collaborations between Singapore and China’s relations going forward! Although I can read and speak conversational mandarin, delivering a whole lecture in Mandarin was definitely a new level and I am happy I took on this challenge and overcome it ☺️☺️

Many thanks to Hong Kong University of Science and Technology for inviting me to speak at this executive training programme ( 国家卫生健康系统高级行政管理干部研修班) entrusted by China’s National Health Commission - thanks Tien Yin Wong 黄天荫 and Nancy Ip for the opportunity! And I missed you too, Donald Low at the programme! Looking forward to future collaborations too.

And yes, many thanks to my team especially Emma Xuxiao Zhang Siew wei Chua Shawn Tan Desmond Sin Sandra Chua Ginny and Shuyi for helping me with the slides and translation too 🙏🏼🙏🏼
