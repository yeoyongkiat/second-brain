🎉 New HSA building groundbreaking completed 👍🎉🥂

Finally we are coming together (soon)! Very happy to witness a very long awaited significant pivotal milestone for Health Sciences Authority yesterday on 7 October 2024, as we gather to break ground for our new HSA building. Honoured to have our Chairman, Prof Benjamin Ong, to grace the ceremony as our Guest of Honour!

This project cements our decade-long of planning, and will now move on to the next phase of automating and digitalising our operations, emboding our dedication to environmental sustainability and innovation and bring together all our HSA colleagues from 5 current different locations into our new building.

Looking forward to working closely with all HSAians and partners in the coming years to build a wonderful new home for HSA - one where HSAians will be proud to call their 2nd home and where they will love to come to interact with each other and with our partners in advancing public health ☺️
