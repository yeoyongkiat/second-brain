Health Sciences Authority is very happy to partner with Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency to introduce this regulatory innovation corridor 🇸🇬 🤝 🇬🇧

Through this partnership, we are engaging earlier, sharing scientific insights, and creating clearer regulatory pathways so promising medical innovations can reach patients faster — without compromising on safety and quality!

I’m grateful to Lawrence Tallon and the MHRA team for their strong collaboration, and to Flagship Pioneering (Noubar Afeyan Junaid Bajwa Andre Andonian Hwee Juin Lee) for being the first industry partner to step forward and help operationalise the details of this regulatory innovation corridor. We look forward to other industry companies joining us in subsequent phases!

And indeed this is what modern regulation should look like — collaborative, science-driven and patient-centred 👍👍

“When regulators work together, patients benefit sooner”
