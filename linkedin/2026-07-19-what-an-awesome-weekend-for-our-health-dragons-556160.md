What an awesome weekend for our Health Dragons and a BIG CONGRATS to the team for a stellar performance ! 🐉🚣‍♀️

A huge shout-out to all our Health Dragons paddlers who competed at the Pesta Sukan and Public Service Dragon Boat races this weekend. We fielded our largest number of teams ever, reached a record number of finals, and celebrated several milestones—including 1st runner-up for Corporate Mixed, 3rd place for Women’s, and 4th place for Men’s Seniors. 💪👏🏆

These achievements reflect not only our strength on the water, but also the commitment, teamwork and camaraderie built through many hours of tough training.

My deepest appreciation to our Captains Feng Ru Yeoh Poh Seng Yeo , EXCO members Clarissa and Yong Chuen, admin coordinators Song Sim Dennys O. and Mas Rizalynda and supporters who worked tirelessly behind the scenes—from organising training and line-ups to keeping everyone motivated and race-ready.

Congratulations, everyone. Rest well, recover fully, and enjoy this special moment. We have much to celebrate—and even more to look forward to as we continue to paddle, grow and succeed together in the next IHH sea regetta! 💪💪
