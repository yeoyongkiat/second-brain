A packed & exhilarating past week with numerous regulatory and cybersecurity overseas experts in SG:
1️⃣Celebrated Centre of Regulatory Excellence 10th anniversary with a 2 day conference
2️⃣Discussed MOH’s regulatory strategy & workplan with esteemed experts in the Regulatory Advisory Panel, RAP
3️⃣Held multiple bilateral meetings with overseas regulatory counterparts on possible collaboration
4️⃣Conducted outcome based collaboration model workshop by Christopher Hodges OBE
5️⃣Launched the world first Cybersecurity Labelling Scheme for Medical Devices together with Health Sciences Authority Synapxe Cyber Security Agency of Singapore (CSA) and discussed way forward for Guidance for MD Cybersecurity together with Global Digital Health Partnership (GDHP)

Very happy that we received *TRACTION* in the regulatory & cybersecurity work with these takeaways:
✅Transformation of regulations across healthcare products, services and data
✅Responsiveness of regulations to new scientific developments, eg AI/genomics
✅Agility in reviewing & adopting regulations eg regulatory sandboxes
✅Collaborativeness with local & global regulatory partners, industry, healthcare professionals & public (Nidhi Swarup)
✅Trust with our stakeholders need to be continually upheld
✅Innovativenees in regulatory policies, operations & engagement
✅Open-minded in hearing ground and team feedback to refine & enhance our regulations
✅Networking with industry & professionals to keep abreast of latest developments

Many thanks to John Lim and Silke Vogel for inviting me on the organising committee & to my “partner-in-crime” Murray Lumpkin for putting up such an awesome 2 day programme 🥳

I will like to thank all speakers, RAP experts, regulatory counterparts & attendees for joining in the conference & discussions & for all your valuable insight over the past 5 days! We look forward to the partnerships 👍

Of cos, a BIG thanks to my teams in HRG, MOH & CoRE for all the efforts in organising the past week of programme so smoothly 👏
