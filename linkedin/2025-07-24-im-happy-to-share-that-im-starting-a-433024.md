I’m happy to share that I’m starting a new position as Chief Executive Officer-Designate at Health Sciences Authority!

I will also be concurrently continuing on with my role as the Deputy Director-General of Health in Health Regulation at the Ministry of Health (Singapore) overseeing the regulations of healthcare services and information. Through this, I aim to better synergise regulatory policies, operations and enforcement across healthcare services, information and products in Singapore across both organisations.

HSA is not an unfamiliar place as I was working there from 2011 to 2016 as the Group Director overseeing health products regulations, so I am definitely looking forward to engaging, collaborating and working with everyone in HSA and our various stakeholders and partners (e.g. industry and its associations, healthcare providers and professionals) to continue advancing the good work that HSA is already doing! 👍
