#1: Proficiency at HSA

At the Health Sciences Authority (HSA), our dedicated firearms examiners are always prepared to assist the Singapore Police Force (SPF) and other agencies in their investigations on shooting-related incidents if and when that should occur. Our commitment to public safety remains strong and ever ready.

Yesterday, I had the opportunity to witness the test-firing of a firearm at our Forensic Chemistry & Physics Laboratory. Maintaining forensic firearms expertise which is a critical national capability, underscores HSA's important role in serving the administration of justice and ensuring the safety of our community through scientific excellence and dedication.

If you are passionate about science and justice, I encourage you to visit our HSA website (https://lnkd.in/gTHpMZPt) to learn more about the fascinating world of forensic science and medicine as well as analytical testing services.

Alaric Koh Ziliang Yang Angeline Yap Health Sciences Authority
