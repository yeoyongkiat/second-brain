This past week was a super busy week but it also reminds me that no matter how busy we are, we are not machines and our body needs a break at times. And that’s what I did - took a long sleep to nurse the high fever and stayed off work emails for a while to clear my mind (so if I am slow in my emails or WA replies, pls pardon me and thanks for all the patience!)

There’s a Chinese saying which goes “休息是为了走更长的路”, i.e. rest to walk a longer distance ahead of us. And as a leader, we should acknowledge that rest is a source of strength (and not a weakness!) and it’s important for the betterment of the organisation and staff well-being.

Nonetheless, I am also very grateful and appreciative of the dedication from these 3 unique but united teams from HSA which worked around my schedule and sent me lots of best wishes to push me on:

1️⃣ Our Chemical Metrology Laboratory signed our 4th MOU, with our counterpart from Vietnam, Vietnam Metrology Institute (VMI), to look at knowledge sharing and exchange, competency and capacity building, so as to enhance the delivery of accurate and high quality lab and clinical services to the people and strengthen metrology in the Asia Pacific region. Big thanks to Dr Ngo Thi Ngoc Ha, Director of VMI for the MOU signing. Tang Lin Teo

2️⃣ Met up with the team from CEPI (Coalition for Epidemic Preparedness Innovations) Adam Hacker JOHN SKERRITT Catherine Hoath Meenu Batolar and had a very fruitful discussion on how HSA can better support an expedited 100-day drug and vaccine development strategy so that we are ready for Disease X. Our conclusion is that we should already start preparing for this during peacetime and not only during epi or pandemics so that we are always ready 👍

3️⃣ HSA held our biennial strategic retreat with Singapore Red Cross to discuss ways in which we need to raise the number of blood donors, particularly our youth donors, to cope with a likely increase in blood demand from our ageing population. We held focus group discussions amongst the teams, including the youth donors too, to brainstorm 3Rs - how to RECRUIT more new blood donors (particularly youth donors) and workplaces to join us in this good cause, how to RETAIN them to be repeat blood donors or to adopt our blood banks to support us further and lastly, how to RECOGNISE the repeat donors and workplaces who have been our fervent supporters! Looking forward to the partnership with SRC Benjamin William Prakash Menon Eileen Cher cos we are thinking out of the box to make these more exciting and meaningful for the donors and workplaces, so stay tuned for more details!
