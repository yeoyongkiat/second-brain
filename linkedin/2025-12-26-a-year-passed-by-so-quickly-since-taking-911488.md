A year passed by so quickly since taking over the helmship of CE of HSA ☺️ As I reflect over the past year, I concluded that it has been both an exhilarating and demanding year — one that required pace, patience and difficult choices — but also a deeply meaningful and fulfilling one.

If I were to give myself a personal report card for the 1st year:
 • Teamwork: 10/10 — everyone in HSA and MOH HReg put in their 💯% best working in regulatory, enforcement, blood collection and supply, forensic sciences and medicine work
 • Fulfilment: 9/10 — from the purpose and impact of HSA’s 5 year 5P strategy roadmap on developing transformative products, platforms, people, partnerships and profiling
 • Fatigue: 9/10 — transformation is energising, but it is also real and relentless catching up with the workload, part time studies and pace of change
 • Conviction: 8/10 — strengthened by experience, reflection and honest conversations with staff and stakeholders

I am very glad that we (I) initiated multiple MOUs and initiatives with global regulatory counterparts, blood and forensic partners, to advance our scientific work and capabilities: (II) launched our own Instagram; (III) rolled out more staff engagement and wellness activities and many more… but what came as a “curve ball” in the past few months was the step up of our vaping enforcement work amidst limited resourcing. So we are very thankful for all the agencies and partners who doubled up to help with enforcement, case management, rehabilitative work - it felt like a “re-live” of COVID-19 where many of us reunited again to fight this “vaping crisis” together day and night 👍

So I am deeply grateful and thankful to all HSA, MOH HReg, V-CMTG, HD & HTT teammates, for your support, professionalism and openness to new ways of working, even while carrying heavy operational loads.

I am also thankful to PSs, DGH, Prof Ben Ong and HSA Board members, whose steady guidance have been invaluable throughout this period.

This past year reminded me as a leader of the importance of pacing ourselves, creating space to recharge, and supporting one another through change.

Looking ahead, 2026 will be a milestone year as HSA marks our 25th Anniversary — a chance not just to celebrate how far we have come, but to reimagine the organisation we must become for the future: firm yet fair, enabling yet principled, trusted locally & globally. So please look forward to more exciting transformative work and stay tuned to them!

To our partners and stakeholders across government, healthcare, industry, community & international networks — thank you for your continued trust and partnership 🙏🏼 and not forgetting my beloved family for their patience and love too 🥰

I step into the new year with humility, renewed energy and belief in what we can build together and looking forward to the collective partnership 💪

I wish everyone and your loved ones a Merry Xmas 🎄 and a Joyous 2026 filled with good health and successes 🎊
