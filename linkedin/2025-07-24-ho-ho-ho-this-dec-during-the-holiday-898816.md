Ho Ho Ho…. This Dec, during the holiday season, HSA and SRC collaborated with Peanuts to launch our first ever thematic blood donation drive 👍 it was an awesome successful campaign launch this morning with lots of donors turning up and enjoying the fringe activities and taking photos with Charlie, Lucy and Snoopy! Kudos to the SRC and HSA organising team!

In HSA, we are often concerned that blood donations typically drop by 20% during the festive season and depending on where one goes, donors may even be ineligible to give blood donations for up to 4 months after the return.

So if you are travelling soon, please consider coming down to any of the blood banks to make a life-saving donation first and you can also receive some exclusive limited edition Peanuts merchandise (while stocks last) after the blood donation 👍👍

Thanks in advance for making a difference and helping to keep our national blood stocks healthy 🙏🏼🙏🏼

#hohohopeofblood and get your friends and family to come down and support too!
