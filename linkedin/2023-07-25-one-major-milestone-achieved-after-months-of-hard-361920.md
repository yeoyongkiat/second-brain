One major milestone achieved 👏👏 After months of hard work by the MOH-HSA-CSA-IHIS teams cochaired with Leon Chang , we are heartened to launch the new Cybersecurity Labelling Regime for Medical Devices (CLS-MD) this week at the Singapore International Cyberweek 2022 👍 this will nudge the MD manufacturers to enhance their cybersecurity posture and allows consumers to choose the degree of security of the MDs against cyber risks!

Did 2 back to back presentations on CLS-MD and the cybersecurity landscape of our healthcare sector at the session and gotten very good feedback on the way forward! A formal industry consultation paper on CLS-MD will be put up in the coming weeks - so do look out for it!
