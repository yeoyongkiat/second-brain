🇸🇬🎉 Happy 60th Birthday, Singapore! 🎉🇸🇬 From all of us @ Health Sciences Authority 🎉🥳🎂

This week, we marked a double first for HSA — our first-ever National Day Observance Ceremony outside the office and the launch of our inaugural HSA Sports Day at the Singapore Sports Hub! 🏟️💪 (big thanks to Sport Singapore Alan Goh for your support and help in hosting our sports day and NDOC!)

From mass workouts to mini-Hyrox team races, colleagues came together to celebrate our nation’s birthday with energy, pride, and plenty of team spirit.

Here’s to One HSA, One Team, One Nation — keeping Singapore and ourselves healthy, safe, and strong, together! ❤️
