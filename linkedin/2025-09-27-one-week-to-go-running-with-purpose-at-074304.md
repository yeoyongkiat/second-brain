One Week to Go: Running with Purpose at the Singtel-SCS Race Against Cancer!

Next Sunday, I’ll be lacing up for the 10KM Race Against Cancer – not just as an individual, but as part of a bigger movement. Together with my colleagues from HSA and the wider public healthcare family, we’re running to support those affected by cancer – our patients, their caregivers, and loved ones!

This race is more than just a physical challenge. It’s a show of solidarity. A reminder that no one fights cancer alone. 💪

We can all play a part. If you’re able, please consider donating to help fund vital programmes and services that support cancer patients in Singapore.

👉 Please donate here: https://lnkd.in/gd-CwB5G

Every contribution—big or small—makes a difference.

Thank you so much for your generosity and encouragement. 🙏🏼 Let’s run this race together—for hope, for healing, and for a future without cancer.
