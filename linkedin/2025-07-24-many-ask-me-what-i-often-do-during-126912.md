Many ask me what I often do during my weekends, besides work and family. So I say I volunteer my services as a wedding solemniser and have been doing so for the past 18 years. Why? Because (1) I believe in giving back to the society and community in whatever capacity and time I have; (2) it’s very gratifying chatting with the newly weds to prepare for their big day and to see their happy smiles before, during and after their solemnisation and (3) a good mental health boost when you make couples happy (and sometimes tickled) during the solemnisation process too!

Today on 22.2, I have the honour of doing so for one of my juniors in the medical profession - Bryan and Ching Yee, who’s the first couple I just solemnised in the year of the Snake - congrats to them and wishing them a splendid and blissful journey ahead!

#happy4newlyweds
