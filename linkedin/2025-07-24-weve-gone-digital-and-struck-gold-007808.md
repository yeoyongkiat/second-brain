We’ve gone digital — and struck gold 🏆

So happy and honoured that Health Sciences Authority received the Gold Award for Best HR Digital Transformation Strategy at the EXA Employee Experience Awards 2025 last night 👏👏💪💪

This milestone celebrates the bold vision and unwavering dedication of Wan Ve Low and our amazing HR team, who reimagined the employee journey to improve their engagement and work experience digitally, for example with Momo — our first HR ChatBot that allows staff easy access to HR information and queries related to benefits, performance and pay.

At HSA, it’s not just about going digital.
We are about putting our employees and processes at the heart of digital transformation. Digital first. People always.

To all our HSA staff particularly those who shared their pain points and did beta testing of the ChatBot — thank you for being on this journey with us! Look out for the launch of Momo at our HSA staff forum on 27 May 👍👍
