“I will be shaving my hair one week later — but together, we can help someone keep their hope.”

This year marks my 8th Hair for Hope, and it is especially meaningful as we celebrate HSA’s 25th Anniversary. Pls support the cancer patients and their families via https://lnkd.in/gR9VkPbt

Every year, shaving my head reminds me that while hair will grow back, many children and patients undergoing cancer treatment face challenges far greater than losing their hair. They battle uncertainty, endure difficult treatments, and yet continue to inspire us with extraordinary courage and resilience.

To every cancer patient and survivor: you are not alone. Your strength gives hope to all of us, and we stand beside you on this journey.

To your families and caregivers: thank you for being the quiet heroes. Your love, sacrifice and unwavering support are often the greatest source of strength during the toughest days.

This year, i hope to raise $25,000 in conjunction with HSA’s 25th Anniversary. It is a small way for us to honour those fighting cancer and to support the work that gives patients and families hope for a better tomorrow.

I thank you in advance for your contribution, no matter the size, sends a powerful message to every patient and family:

You are not fighting this battle alone. Together, we can turn compassion into hope, and hope into strength.

Because while hair grows back, hope grows stronger when we stand together 🙂
