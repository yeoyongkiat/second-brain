How can HSA not be part of the Hyrox Fanatic this weekend? 😜

This is my third year participating in Hyrox and this year, 5 teams (4 from HSA and 1 from MOH) joined me in the HYROX arena to take on both the men’s and women’s relay races! (Plus two other single racers Shalini and Patricvk!!)

For many, it was their first-ever HYROX race, so we trained hard, and even spruced up the HSA gym to push ourselves further.

The results?
1. Our Under-40 HSA-MOH Women’s Team clinched 2nd place with an impressive 1:21 timing — making it to the podium!
2. The Above-40 MOH Men’s Team came in 4th at 1:27 timing — just 1 minute shy of a podium finish!

Nonetheless, I was glad everyone completed the race safely, and just as importantly, we kept our spirits high. We wrapped the evening with a celebratory dinner, filled with laughs and excited chats about future races! Thanks to our coach Patricvk for the tough training and our supporter Alaric Koh too!

Now that we’ve caught the HYROX fever, we’re all in — not just for fitness only, but also for building a workplace that’s closer, happier and healthier. Because when we train together, support one another, we strengthen not just our bodies — but the very fabric of our organisation and teams!!

Here’s to more races, more resilience, and a shared mission to make HSA and MOH the healthy place to grow, thrive and move — together!
