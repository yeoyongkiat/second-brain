Happy to attend Singapore Red Cross (SRC) 75th Anniversary Benefit Gala Dinner last evening! Thanks for the invite 🙏🏼 and kudos to the organising team, SRC staff and volunteers for making the gala dinner and fund raising event a huge success!!

Many thanks to the many years of close partnership between HSA and SRC and for all their hard work and efforts in organising community and educational outreach, donor recruitment and blood donation drives, so as to ensure a stable, safe, and adequate blood supply for Singapore 👍👏
