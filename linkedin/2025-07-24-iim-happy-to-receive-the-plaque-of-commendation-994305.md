II’m Happy to receive the Plaque of Commendation on behalf of HSA last evening at the NTUC May Day Awards 2025 ceremony 💪👏👍

This Plaque of Commendation epitomises HSA’s commitment to the professional growth and physical and mental well-being of all our employees! This was only possible through the strong partnership with our union, AUSBE, including regular management-union dialogue sessions where we actively discuss and co-create initiatives to address ground concerns and make positive impact to employee well-being.

This was also possible through the strong support of and collaboration with the Union by my predecessor, Dr Mimi Choong, who has been conferred the 'Friend of Labour' award too! Kudos to her past years of hard work and labour!

We look forward to continuing this meaningful partnership with AUSBE in the years ahead and to creating a conducive and engaging environment and culture for all HSA employees 👍👏☺️

Health Sciences Authority Mimi May_Ling Choong
