💪 Team Up. Adopt a Bloodbank. Save Lives. Any volunteers keen to sign up? 😉

Yesterday, Health Sciences Authority and Singapore Red Cross launched our refreshed “Adopt a Bloodbank” initiative — empowering workplaces and organisations to play an even greater role in sustaining Singapore’s national blood supply:
1. Bosses lead by example as donors.
2. Employees feel inspired and engaged to give.
3. Organisations can collaborate with their adopted bloodbanks — showcasing their brand, products, hosting donation drives, and fostering a culture of care and giving.

Any organisations keen to volunteer, pls look for me or sign up here at https://lnkd.in/gpHech8x

Through our new four-tier recognition framework and Blood Partner Award, we’re celebrating the workplaces and individuals who make regular giving a way of life.

As I shared at the launch — partnerships are the pulse of our blood supply. By coming together, we build not just a system that saves lives, but a society that cares. ❤️

We also marked the 10th anniversary of Bloodbank@Westgate Tower (BB@WT) — a community beacon that has collected over 215,000 units of blood, contributing about 17% of Singapore’s national supply.

Behind every drop lies the dedication of our teams, volunteers, and the many donors who give selflessly and regularly. Thank you for keeping Singapore’s lifeline flowing strong. 🩸
