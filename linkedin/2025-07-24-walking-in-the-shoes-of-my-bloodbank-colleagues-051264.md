🩸 Walking in The Shoes of My Bloodbank Colleagues 😊

In honour of World Blood Donor Day tomorrow, I decided to step into the shoes of our incredibly dedicated Bloodbank team — experiencing firsthand what it takes to keep Singapore’s blood supply flowing safely and reliably.

From the moment a donor walks through our doors, it’s a coordinated effort involving all our clinical nurses, medical screeners, and assistants — each playing a vital role. I followed their journey from registration and haemoglobin testing to medical screening to collection and finally packing the donated blood for testing and storage.

What struck me most was their unwavering commitment to donor care and the meticulous safety standards upheld at every step. It’s a powerful reminder of the unseen heroes working behind the scenes in our bloodbanks — and the generous donors who make it all possible.

🙏 Many thanks to all our donors and Bloodbank staff — your dedication saves lives, every single day. So I want to express my deepest gratitude to each and every donor — past, present, and future. You don’t just give blood; you give second chances.

Let’s go one step further.
💡 If you’ve donated before — come back again.
💡 If you’ve never donated — now is the perfect time.
💡 If you know someone eligible — bring them along.

Together, we can build a stronger, more resilient blood supply — one donor, one community, many lifesavers!
