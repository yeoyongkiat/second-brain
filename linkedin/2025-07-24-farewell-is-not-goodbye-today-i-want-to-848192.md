Farewell is not goodbye! Today I want to say a BIG thanks to an exceptional leader, Dr Mimi Choong, Chief Executive Officer of Health Sciences Authority (HSA), who has positioned HSA as a global trusted authority, particularly in advancing scientific innovation and regulatory excellence.

It is not possible to capture in words the depth of our gratitude for her decade of extraordinary leadership and achievements. Some notable examples include HSA becoming one of the first three regulators worldwide to achieve WHO Listed Authority status for medicines in 2023, following the prestigious WHO Maturity Level 4 designation in 2022. She also strengthened HSA’s international collaborations, for example, in regulatory work sharing initiatives under an ACCESS Consortium with Australia, Canada, Switzerland, and the UK, Asia Pacific Blood Network and Asian Forensic Sciences Network. Mimi has been also been steadfast in championing transformation, digitalisation and excellence has also made HSA more efficient, resilient and forward-looking, to be ready for the challenges of the future.

I have also benefitted from her support, guidance and leadership from 2014 to 2016 when I was then the Group Director of Health Products Regulation Group before I moved on to MOH in 2017. Her foresight, strategic organisational outlook, strong networks wirh stakeholders, meticulous attention to policy trade-offs and operations, and eye to details taught me many important leadership lessons, including how to better empower, coach and mentor staff to do their best for the organisation.

I definitely have a big shoe to fill as I take over from Mimi tomorrow, but I am heartened that HSA is a strong and well-run organisation with a talented, dedicated and passionate team of HSA-ians.

On behalf of all HSA-ians, I wish you a very happy and fulfilling retirement and have now more time to travel and pick up some hobbies. We continue to welcome your input as we undergo our regulatory transformation review and will always consider you part of the HSA family. So please come back to visit us often and when the new HSA building is completed.

THANK YOU VERY MUCH MIMI !
