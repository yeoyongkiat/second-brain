# Raymond Chua

> Chief Executive Officer, **Health Sciences Authority (HSA)** · concurrently **Deputy Director-General of Health (Health Regulation), Ministry of Health (Singapore)**

*A profile synthesised from 50 LinkedIn posts (July 2022 → July 2026) and his November 2025 SMA News interview [[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]. Every claim links back to the source it was drawn from.*

## Summary

Raymond Chua is a Singapore healthcare regulator and CEO who blends serious, framework-driven regulatory strategy with an unusually warm, people-first leadership style. A medical doctor who trained as a public health physician, he took a detour into the pharmaceutical industry before being called back to become — in his own words — an "accidental" regulator ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]). He transformed HSA's product regulation, then MOH's health-services regulation, and returned to HSA as its Chief Executive — while continuing to hold a concurrent MOH deputy director-general role. His guiding philosophy is regulation that *enables* rather than restrains. He is a globally active voice on the responsible regulation of AI in healthcare, a relentless champion of Singapore's blood supply, and — off the clock — a HYROX athlete, dragonboater, cause-fundraiser, and ~20-year volunteer wedding solemniser.

His public voice is upbeat and appreciative to a fault: heavy on emojis, hashtags, named shout-outs to staff and partners, and a recurring insistence that "the future of HSA is not mine alone to shape."

## Career trajectory

*The fuller picture comes from the SMA News interview ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]); it also corrects the loose dates in the LinkedIn posts — he returned to HSA as Group Director in **2012**, not 2011/2014.*

- **Early career** — trained as a medical doctor; intended to specialise in **O&G** (sat the Part One exam) before a 2000 MOPEX posting to MOH's Hospital Services Division redirected him into **public health** ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).
- **2002** — **Master of Public Health, London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine**; later added an **MBA (University of Nottingham)** after finding he lacked economics/management grounding. Rotated through MOH divisions (healthcare finance, epidemiology & disease control, clinical quality & patient safety) and the newly set-up MOH Holdings ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).
- **~2007–2012 — private sector.** Managing Director at **Eisai Clinical Research Singapore** (started three clinical-research offices — Singapore, Beijing, Mumbai — bringing global trials to Asia), then **International Medical Director at Shire Pharmaceuticals** ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]). This "regulatee" stint shaped his enabling philosophy.
- **2012** — recruited back by then-HSA CEO Prof John Lim ("come back to the bright side") as **Group Director, Health Products Regulation, HSA** — the "accidental regulator." Weathered the 2012 medical-device regulatory crisis and transformed products regulation ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]], [[2025-07-24-farewell-is-not-goodbye-today-i-want-to-848192]]).
- **2017 onward** — moved to **MOH Health Regulation Group**, which he led ([[2025-07-24-farewell-is-not-goodbye-today-i-want-to-848192]], [[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]). Key deliverables:
  - Revamped the archaic premises-based **Private Hospitals and Medical Clinics Act (PHMC, 1980)** into a **services-based** framework enabling telemedicine and Mobile Inpatient Care @ Home ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).
  - Drove the **Healthcare Services Act (HCSA)** rollout and its Phase 2 service-specific consultation, starting with the dental community ([[2023-07-25-finally-kickstarted-our-consultation-yesterday-on-the-healthcare-221440]]).
  - Handled COVID-19 regulatory work alongside BAU ([[2023-07-25-dear-friends-near-and-far-as-2022-comes-646016]]).
  - Co-chaired the world-first **Cybersecurity Labelling Scheme for Medical Devices (CLS-MD)**, launched at Singapore International Cyberweek 2022 ([[2023-07-25-one-major-milestone-achieved-after-months-of-hard-361920]]).
  - Championed a regulatory philosophy of *enabling* innovative healthcare, not merely safeguarding it, and helped spearhead an Asia-Pacific network of healthcare regulators ([[2023-07-25-it-was-great-sharing-over-the-past-month-138176]]).
- **2022** — graduated **Harvard Business School's 7-week Advanced Management Programme (AMP)**, alongside 160+ leaders from 45 countries ([[2022-07-25-yay-finally-graduated-from-a-7-week-advanced-780672]]).
- **December 2024** — became **CEO-Designate → CEO of HSA**, succeeding Dr Mimi Choong, while retaining the MOH DDGH role ([[2025-07-24-at-my-first-day-of-work-i-posted-928384]], [[2025-07-24-farewell-is-not-goodbye-today-i-want-to-848192]], [[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).
- Pursuing **part-time studies** concurrently with the CE role ([[2025-12-26-a-year-passed-by-so-quickly-since-taking-911488]]). Still holds his **practising certificate** "just in case" ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).

## Areas of focus

### Healthcare regulation & transformation
The through-line of his career: regulation that *enables* innovation while safeguarding safety. He frames HSA's tripartite mission as **safeguarding public health, securing the nation's blood supply, and serving the administration of justice** ([[2025-07-24-as-i-mark-this-significant-milestone-i-will-025216]]), and set out a **5-year "5P" strategy roadmap — products, platforms, people, partnerships and profiling** ([[2025-12-26-a-year-passed-by-so-quickly-since-taking-911488]]). His signature tools are **regulatory sandboxes**, close stakeholder engagement, and "horizon scanning" so regulators don't lag behind innovation — recounted at length in the SMA interview, along with the formative 2012 medical-device crisis that taught him "regulation is not just a science but an art — an art of communication" ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]).

### Digital health & the Health Information Bill (HIB)
Rolling out the **Health Information Bill** to mandate that licensed providers contribute key data to the **National Electronic Health Record (NEHR)** for continuity of care — with patient opt-out access restrictions, "break the glass" emergency access, access logs on HealthHub, a whitelist of compliant IT vendors, grants/training, and a sunrise period ahead of the Bill's first reading in Q1 2026 ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]). Originally slated with the HCSA in 2020 but delinked and delayed after the 2018 SingHealth cybersecurity breach.

### Precision medicine & genomics
Working to encourage genetic testing for early detection while guarding against discrimination — a **2021 moratorium** barring insurers from acquiring genomic information, now being considered for legislation making such acquisition (by insurers or employers) a punishable offence ([[2025-11-shaping-the-future-of-healthcare-regulation-raymond-chua]]). Also watching longevity/wellness services that make non-evidence-based claims.

## Voice & personality

Warm, upbeat and relentlessly appreciative — generous with emojis, exclamation marks, hashtags, and named shout-outs. Playful and pop-culture-savvy (Gandalf, Squid Games, Snoopy, the "good regulation is like underpants" quip), and gently self-deprecating ("pardon this old man trying to sync the dance moves"). Underneath the warmth is a structured strategist who thinks in frameworks and is candid about pressure and fatigue. Humble on the world stage — *"we are very small but hopefully we are value creating"* ([[2026-05-25-my-5cs-of-global-regulatory-collaboration-and-hope-125824]]) — and consistently one of the team rather than above it ("One HSA," "all of us," "HSA-ians").
