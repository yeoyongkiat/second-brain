# How Raymond Speaks — Tone, Policy Lexicon & Vision

*Synthesised from all 12 of his CEO speeches (January 2025 → June 2026). Every point backlinks to the speech(es) it is drawn from. The full index of speeches is at the [bottom](#speech-index).*

> In a line: **the enabling regulator, cast in warm partnership language and vivid metaphor — a referee "who understands the game," whose recurring proof-point is trust, and whose every mechanism is subordinated to the patient.**

---

## 1 · Tone & Rhetorical Style

**Overall voice.** Warm, aspirational, consultative and relentlessly partnership-oriented — a "regulator-who-is-not-a-bureaucrat," technically fluent but consciously non-adversarial. His register flexes by audience: statesmanlike and gratitude-laden at anniversaries ([[2025-11-01-bloodbank-westgate-tower-10th-year-anniversary-cel|Bloodbank@Westgate 10th]], [[2026-04-15-80th-anniversary-of-the-national-blood-programme|NBP 80th]]), pastoral and emotionally raw on vaping ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]]), and energetic and salesmanlike when courting investors ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).

### Signature rhetorical moves

- **The "enabler, not inhibitor" antithesis — his single most pervasive device.** "we're not just regulating the present—we're enabling the future" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]); "governance is not a brake on innovation — it is an accelerator" ([[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]]); "Will regulation be the enabler that accelerates innovation, or will it become the inhibitor that holds back progress?" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); "not just as a regulator, but as a collaborator and enabler" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Sports / championship analogy.** The extended World Cup metaphor casting the ecosystem as a football team — innovators as strikers, clinicians midfielders, manufacturers defenders, VCs club owners, government league organisers, and "at HSA, we are proud to play the role of the referee… not a referee who constantly blows the whistle and stops the game—but one who understands the game" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); radiology's "new golden age" ([[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]]).
- **Journey / bridge / pathway metaphors.** "a bridge between our institutions but a pathway to a brighter future… a shared journey of excellence" ([[2025-01-20-mou-signing-between-hsa-and-vietnam-metrology-inst|VMI MOU]]); conference theme "Breaking Barriers and Building Bridges," radiologists as "the bridge between technology and patient trust" ([[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]]); "HSA is ready to walk alongside you" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Coined, repeatable acronym frameworks.** The **3C Framework — CHECK · CHOOSE · CALL OUT** ("Your teeth are not a fashion accessory") ([[2026-04-17-international-dental-exhibition-and-meeting-idem|IDEM]]); the **AIHGle** guidelines "pronounced 'agile'" pun ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); the **Three Pillars** — Global Market Access, Holistic Value Proposition, Adaptive Regulation ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **The digital-twin theatrical open.** Revealing that the preceding video was his AI digital twin — "What you have just listened to is my digital twin speaking to you about a scenario from the future" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); a digital twin of a clinician "conducting consultation in ways that were unimaginable" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Storytelling / case vignettes — his most powerful emotional tool.** The two contrasting 16-year-old vaping cases (the girl rejected by her family vs. the girl whose father "became her anchor"); "Wei Qiang's journey" ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]]); the self-taught "dentist" operating "out of hotel rooms," veneers bought online falling off "within days" ([[2026-04-17-international-dental-exhibition-and-meeting-idem|IDEM]]); named frontline staff as heroes ([[2025-11-01-bloodbank-westgate-tower-10th-year-anniversary-cel|Bloodbank@Westgate 10th]]).
- **Direct address to each stakeholder segment in turn** — "To the innovators in this room… To the industry leaders… To the clinicians in the room: your voices do matter too" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]); "To our youth… You didn't choose addiction—but you can choose recovery" ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]]).
- **Proverbs & quotations for gravitas.** A Vietnamese proverb ("One tree alone cannot make a mountain…") and Ho Chi Minh ("The health of the people is the strength of the country") ([[2025-01-20-mou-signing-between-hsa-and-vietnam-metrology-inst|VMI MOU]]); "good regulators are the architects of the future" ([[2026-03-10-29th-session-international-medical-device-regulato|IMDRF Forum]]).
- **Triads and rhetorical questions.** "predictive, personalised and precise" ([[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]]); "from punishment to include healing, from isolation to include connection, and from despair to include hope" ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]]); "The question isn't *if* healthcare will transform but *whether* our regulatory frameworks can enable that transformation" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]).
- **Occasional disarming humour / pop-culture.** A Mission: Impossible AI reference — "I won't provide more spoilers here" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]); "since we are in the World Cup mood now" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).

### How he opens and closes
- **Opens** with formal salutations to dignitaries, a warm "very warm welcome to Singapore," then a hook — a proverb, a movie, or the digital-twin reveal. He often flags "I wear several hats" to establish his dual MOH/HSA authority ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]], [[2026-04-17-international-dental-exhibition-and-meeting-idem|IDEM]]).
- **Closes** with a signature valediction — "I wish all of you a productive and insightful [days] ahead" — and a vision-laden "**Together, let us…**" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]), often reducing everything to the patient: "our greatest victory is not lifting a trophy. It is giving every patient the opportunity to live a healthier, longer and better life" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).

### Refrains that recur across multiple speeches
- **"Co-create"** the regulations/solutions — [[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]], [[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]].

- **"AI is not here to replace… but to empower / amplify"** — [[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]], [[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]].
- **"We cannot build this future alone."** — [[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]], [[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]].

## 2 · Policy & Initiative Lexicon

The concrete, named things he reaches for. Grouped; each tagged with the speech(es) it appears in.

### Regulatory schemes, pathways & routes
- **Pre-market Consultation Scheme** — launched 2017, "over 30 consultations a year" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Priority Review / priority & abridged routes** ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Class B Immediate Registration Route** — fast-track for low-to-medium-risk devices ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]).
- **Change Management Program (CMP) for SaMD** — pre-specify software/algorithm changes without full re-evaluation ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]]).
- **AI-SaMD regulatory sandbox** (Feb 2026) and **regulatory sandboxes** generally — telemedicine, community-pharmacist influenza vaccination ([[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]).
- **AI-enabled Medical Device Risk Classification Tool** — HSA's own AI tool, launched in beta ([[2026-03-10-29th-session-international-medical-device-regulato|IMDRF Forum]]).

### Frameworks & guidelines
- **AIHGle** (AI in Healthcare Guidelines, "pronounced 'agile'") — first launched 2021 (MOH/HSA/Synapxe) → **AIHGle 2.0**, published March 2026 ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]], [[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]], [[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **Regulatory Guidelines for AI Medical Devices** — HSA "among the first globally, in 2019" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-08-29-ams-sgcr-wires-2025|AMS-SGCR-WIRES]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]).
- **Cybersecurity Labelling Scheme for Medical Devices — CLS(MD)** — "global first," with CSA + Synapxe ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]).
- **Right-Touch Regulation** toward a **one-stop health regulatory hub**, and the **world-first Modes of Service Delivery (MOSD)** under HCSA ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]).
- **Risk-based / risk-proportionate framework**; **Complementary Health Products review** (regs ~mid-2028) ([[2026-02-26-nus-academy-of-health-longevity-2026-geromedicine-|Geromedicine]], [[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]]).

### Legislation
- **Health Products Act 2007**; **Healthcare Services Act (HCSA)** (modular, premises-neutral, replacing the Private Hospitals and Medical Clinics Act); **Dental Registration Act** ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]], [[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]], [[2026-04-17-international-dental-exhibition-and-meeting-idem|IDEM]]).

### WHO recognitions (his favourite credibility proof-points)
- **WHO Maturity Level 4 — medical devices**, "first national regulatory authority worldwide," via the **Global Benchmarking Tool Plus**, March 2026 ([[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]], [[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).
- **WHO Maturity Level 4 — medicines** (since 2022); **WHO-Listed Authority** (among the first 3, since 2023); **WHO Stringent Regulatory Authority** for high-risk IVDs ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]], [[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]).

## 3 · Vision for HSA

Remarkably consistent across every speech, reducible to seven themes.

1. **The enabling regulator, not a gatekeeper.** The central plank. "we are not just regulating today's healthcare system – we're enabling tomorrow's. A system where care follows patients rather than requiring patients to follow care" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); the referee who "allows innovation to flow with confidence towards the ultimate goal" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); "good regulators are the architects of the future and smart regulators enable the future to arrive safely" ([[2026-03-10-29th-session-international-medical-device-regulato|IMDRF Forum]]).
2. **A global reference authority and trusted gateway.** "We have transformed from referencing other agencies to becoming a reference agency that other agencies can rely on" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); "Singapore is becoming a high-trust regulatory gateway… with HSA's approval, your products will in effect get a global seal of confidence" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); "a trusted global reference point for other regulators" ([[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]]).
3. **Trust as HSA's foundational asset.** "our most valuable asset — trust… earned the only way it can be earned: through processes that are rigorous, transparent, and consistent" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); "transparency builds trust and trust reinforces the system" ([[2026-04-17-international-dental-exhibition-and-meeting-idem|IDEM]]); rooted early in "fostering trust through science" ([[2025-01-20-mou-signing-between-hsa-and-vietnam-metrology-inst|VMI MOU]]).
4. **Patient-centred — the patient as the ultimate goal.** "we are all playing for something far greater. We are playing for the patient… our greatest victory… is giving every patient the opportunity to live a healthier, longer and better life" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); AI as "a tool in service of better outcomes for the patients who trust us with their care" ([[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]]).
5. **Ecosystem partner / co-creator — "shifting left."** "can we move beyond traditional compliance to become active partners in fostering innovation ecosystems?" ([[2025-10-16-2025-amchamsg-healthcare-conference|AmCham]]); "HSA is deliberately shifting left, to be present at the earliest stages… the best regulation is not written from a distance" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); "regulation is not about imposing rules but about co-creating solutions" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]).
6. **Progressive, agile, anticipatory / future-proof.** "We're not just reacting to change but anticipating it… future-proof our regulations" ([[2025-05-21-official-opening-of-healthtech-x-asia-2025|HealthTech X Asia]]); "a global leader in the responsible regulation of AI-enabled medical technologies" ([[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]); a "progressive adopter" using AI internally ([[2026-03-10-29th-session-international-medical-device-regulato|IMDRF Forum]]).
7. **Firm-but-caring; trusted, not loudest; a life-saving public mission.** "a firm regulator that is tough on vaping… but we are also a caring one" ([[2026-01-30-society-of-behavioural-health-annual-scientific-me|Behavioural Health]]); "we may not aim to be the loudest voice in longevity innovation. But we should be among the most trusted" ([[2026-02-26-nus-academy-of-health-longevity-2026-geromedicine-|Geromedicine]]); "every single donation has the power to save up to three lives" ([[2026-04-15-80th-anniversary-of-the-national-blood-programme|NBP 80th]]).

**The vision, in his own words:**
> "at HSA, we are proud to play the role of the referee. Not a referee who constantly blows the whistle and stops the game—but one who understands the game, applies the rules with fairness, consistency and integrity, protects every player, and allows innovation to flow with confidence towards the ultimate goal." — [[2026-06-29-singapore-medical-device-venture-showcase|MedTech Venture]]

> "When we get governance right, we do not slow innovation. Rather we make it sustainable, trustworthy, and scalable." — [[2026-05-21-pre-conference-pet-ct-workshop|PET/CT Workshop]]

> "we may not aim to be the loudest voice in longevity innovation. But we should be among the most trusted." — [[2026-02-26-nus-academy-of-health-longevity-2026-geromedicine-|Geromedicine]]
